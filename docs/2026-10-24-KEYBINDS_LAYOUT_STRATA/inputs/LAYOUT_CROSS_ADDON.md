# Item 020 — cross-addon reconciliation of the settings-layout proposals

Inputs: the 15 reviewed per-addon files in `inputs/layout/`, `WowAddonStandards/standards/standards/options-ui.md`
(§5-§19), and `LibKa0s/docs/api/Options/version-29.2.34.2.5.9.1.7.4.2.1-docs.md` (the composers, `RenderTabbedSchema`
`opts.tabs`, `PageBanner`, `PageHeader`, `NavRail`). The owner's rules apply throughout. Rows may move, regroup and be
relabelled. Setting keys, defaults, ranges, behaviour and the set of settings do not change. Consistency across the
collection is a goal in its own right.

How the variants were chosen: where options-ui mandates a form, that form wins. Otherwise the variant most addons
already ship (or the per-addon files most often proposed) wins, and the tie-breaker is the variant that changes the
fewest shipped strings. Each convention lists the addons it touches. Confidence is the chance that the convention is
right for the collection, not the chance that one addon's change is right.

## Change-id collision (fix before anything else)

`PrettyChat.md` and `PremadeGroupsFilterExtension.md` both use the prefix `PRE-` (`PRE-L01` exists in both files).
This document uses **`PRC-`** for PrettyChat and keeps `PRE-` for PremadeGroupsFilterExtension. The execution
plan's `items.tsv` should do the same, so a commit subject `PRE-L01: ...` can only mean one addon.

## 2. Overrides

`supersede` means the convention replaces the change and the per-addon change is dropped. `modify` means the change
lands in the altered form given. `add` means a new change the conventions require that the addon's file did not
propose. New ids are `<PFX>-Lxx-new<n>`.
