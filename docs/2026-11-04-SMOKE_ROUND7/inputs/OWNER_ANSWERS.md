# Owner answers, round 7 (2026-11-04)

**Q: PGFE's new mode while the skin picker is open?** Answer: "/pgfe test, panel alone (Recommended)".
That option read: shows the panel on its own, floating in the middle of the screen with your current filters, so the
skin preview has something to paint. Apply is greyed out because there's no PGF dialog to search with. It closes when
the picker closes. Opening the Group Finder isn't involved.
