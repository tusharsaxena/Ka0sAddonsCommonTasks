# BankLedger: skin-defaults audit (LibKa0s#97)

Adopted: `core/SkinSetup.lua` registers owner `BankLedger` at file load. The addon has no HUD (rollout exception BL-03) and no fromSkin companions. `tests/skin_facts.lua` answers both kit gates. `Kit.skinValues.rows` is empty, and `calls` registers `NS.SkinBar`. `Kit.skinSwitch` declares 40 surfaces. Three gaps in the guard: the ranked-bar fill, the stacked segments and the back-to-back sides are not declared. The split-bar surface answers the whole bar, painted in a token color, so it passes on its hue and its labels and never checks the texture. No gate can see the row-tint alpha. Audited at master 3205b7f, read-only, with `libs/` and `tests/_kit/` excluded.
