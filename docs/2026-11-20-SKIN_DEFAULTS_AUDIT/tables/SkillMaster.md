# SkillMaster: skin-defaults audit (LibKa0s#97)

Adopted (owner registration at `core/SkinSetup.lua:226-231`, master `21a93dd`, vendored LibKa0s v1.107.0). Guard: `Kit.skinValues = {}` (`tests/run.lua:62`, no allow rows) and `Kit.skinSwitch.surfaces` with 9 surfaces (main window, detached block, merged window, journal, trainer popup, alert toast, row tooltip bar, broker feed tooltip in the default `window` style, debug console). Not declared: the waypoint arrow, the broker tooltip's `classic` style, the settings-panel BoxList rows, the GroupsSnap glow and ghost, and the Detached style with its companions off. SkillMaster draws no StatusBar. Every data bar is LibKa0s-Widgets-1.0's texture `Bar`, whose fill goes through `Skin:For(owner).BarTexture` while `texture = nil` (`libs/LibKa0s/WidgetsBar.lua:232-246`). A check for `SetStatusBarTexture` alone would miss these bars, but they do follow the skin.
