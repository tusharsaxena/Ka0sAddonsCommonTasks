# PartyFrameEnhanced: skin-defaults audit (LibKa0s#97)

Adopted, at master 9f8147c. The guard is `test_skin_values` plus `test_skin_switch`, both green. `Kit.skinSwitch.surfaces` (tests/skin_facts.lua) declares 27 surfaces: 8 each for the cast bar, the target frame and the pet frame (fill, bar texture, background, border style, border color, text color, face and shadow), plus the spell icon edge (also checked with the Scope ignored), the holder plate and the holder caption. The "bar texture" surface records the key passed to `NS.FetchMedia("statusbar", ...)` and does not tap `SetStatusBarTexture`. That gap is in coverage only: the live path at modules/Element.lua:168-172 passes the fetched texture straight through. The HUD bars do take the skin's statusbar texture, so Combat Trail's failure does not happen here.

## Owner questions

1. **Preview stand-in (E-PF-3).** The stand-in imitates the Blizzard or EllesmereUI party frame, so offsets tuned against it match a real party. Should it keep the host's look under every skin? Recommendation: yes. The frame it copies is never skinned (§7/§9 game art), and a skinned stand-in would mislead the tuning. Rule it as a §9 exception class ("an addon-drawn stand-in for a client or foreign frame, during preview") and re-cite the five StandIn rows from A7 to that ruling. As a small addon fix, paint the `(test)` mark with `text.muted`, because it is the addon's own annotation and not part of the imitation. Note that the stand-in is what the skin picker's unlock preview shows (§10), so under this ruling it will not change when the player picks a skin. Only the attached elements will.
