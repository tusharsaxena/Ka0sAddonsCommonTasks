Adopted (`Skin:RegisterOwner` at core/SkinSetup.lua, owner tokens `at.barFill`/`at.barBg`/`at.barBorder`), master @ 030d6e9. AbsorbTracker has no Results, Compare or Timeline surfaces; the Combat Trail texture trigger maps to the HUD fill and background only, and both hold.
