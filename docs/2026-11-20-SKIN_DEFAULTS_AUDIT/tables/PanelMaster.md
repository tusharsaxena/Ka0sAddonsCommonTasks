## Question for the owner

New-panel look defaults (the UNCLEAR row). R-2 says an option whose default is the addon's own value must follow the skin, with "a stored pick stays". A8 says PanelMaster panels keep the colors and textures the player chose. PanelMaster copies the whole template into each record at creation (Registry.lua:453), so a panel nobody styled cannot be told from one somebody did. Should a brand-new panel be born following the skin (bg, border, bar.texture, bar.border through the library's `fromSkin` companions, new panels only), or do the shipped defaults count as the player's content under A8?

Recommendation: keep A8 and record it as OVERRIDE. A panel is a piece of the player's layout, the addon is explicit that nothing may restyle it unasked (Constants.lua:428-436), and existing panels could not move under R-2 anyway. If you want R-2 here, it is an addon-only change: `fromSkin = true` on the editor groups, with the companions seeded on for new panels only.
