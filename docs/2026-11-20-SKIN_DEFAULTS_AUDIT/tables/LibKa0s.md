# LibKa0s (feat/2026-11-20-libka0s-94-97-99-100 @ 140d56df, v1.107.0)

Not an adopter: the library registers no owner of its own, and each widget reaches a skin only through the owner its host passes (owner-less draws Ka0s Default). Guard: `Kit.skinSwitch` is tests/fixture_skin_switch.lua, three fixture surfaces (S.Panel, S.Text, S.Card scope=panel) and none of the library's widgets; `Kit.skinValues` is unset (declared skip, tests/run.lua:128-129). `F.env()` (tests/fixture_skin_paint.lua:42-52) is called with no `lsm`, so `P.media("statusbar", ...)` answers nil and S.BarTexture paints WHITE8X8 under both Ka0s Default and the probe: a texture check cannot fail today. The probe already gives `bar.texture` another skin's key (testkit/skin_switch.lua:78, `otherValue`), so SD-LK-GUARD needs (a) an LSM fake mapping each skin's statusbar key to its own path, (b) the check to compare the texture path, not only the recorded colors, and (c) declared library surfaces: a Bar, a Charts bar row, a TestStrip, a Footer, a DragHandle and a Dropdown row. Line numbers are at HEAD; the tree carries the uncommitted SD-LK-94 edit to Widgets.lua (+3 lines after :137).
