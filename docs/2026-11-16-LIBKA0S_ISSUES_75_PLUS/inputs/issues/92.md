# #92 Tooltip placement surface (v2.112.0): build the placers, move the library's widgets, adopt in every addon

## What
WowAddonStandards **v2.112.0** adds the tooltip placement rules. They live in standalone-windows "Tooltip placement", launcher-§7, frame-layering-§4 and anti-pattern #119. The standard names a **LibKa0s tooltip placement** surface. Until a LibKa0s tag ships it, addon adoption is blocked, and a host-placed tooltip that already follows the rules is compliant. This issue covers building that surface, moving the library's own widgets onto it, and adopting it in every addon.

### The rules (summary; the standard is normative)
- **Never on the cursor and never at a corner.** No `ANCHOR_CURSOR`, `ANCHOR_CURSOR_RIGHT`, cursor tracking, or `ANCHOR_RIGHT`/`ANCHOR_TOPRIGHT` corner offsets.
- **Body elements** (rows, cells, cards, controls inside rows, column headers, scroll bars, step-panel controls, HUD bars and icons; anything not named as chrome):
  - The tooltip opens **beside the window group**, which includes docked companions, or beside the whole HUD container.
  - It opens on the right when it fits, else on the left: `TOPLEFT` on the group's right edge + `TIP_GAP`, or `TOPRIGHT` on its left edge − `TIP_GAP`.
  - Its top is level with the hovered element. It never covers the group.
  - A companion panel's own tooltips open on its side away from the main window.
  - If neither side fits, the tooltip falls back to centered below the element.
- **Chrome elements** (title bar and controls, test strip, tabs, toolbar and filter bar with dropdowns, search and buttons, footer lines and marks, resize grip, modal dialog controls):
  - The tooltip is **attached and horizontally centered**.
  - **Below** top-chrome elements (`TOP` on the element's `BOTTOM`, 0, −`TIP_GAP`) and **above** footer elements (`BOTTOM` on the element's `TOP`, 0, +`TIP_GAP`).
  - It flips only when the screen has no room.
- **Gap:** `TIP_GAP` = **2 px**, the same in every skin.
- **Launcher** (minimap button): attached to the button, opening toward the screen center. The vertical side comes from the screen half and the horizontal from the screen third. LDB display addons keep their own anchor. LibDBIcon's current 0 px is allowed only until the re-vendor of the tag that ships the placer.
- **Out of scope:** widgets on Settings pages in Blizzard's canvas, and tooltips added to Blizzard's own tooltips.

## Library work
- [ ] The placement surface: a beside-the-window placer, which takes a group of frames and handles the flip and the clamp; an attached-centered placer (below or above, flipping); a launcher placer; and `TIP_GAP = 2`. Add tests, the API doc, a changelog entry and a minor bump.
- [ ] Move the library's own widgets onto it. The v2.112.0 release review found these:
  - `ANCHOR_RIGHT` in IconBrowser.lua, WidgetsDropdown.lua, WidgetsReorder.lua and SimPanel.lua;
  - `ANCHOR_CURSOR` and `ANCHOR_TOP` in WidgetsDragHandle.lua;
  - `ANCHOR_CURSOR_RIGHT` and `ANCHOR_TOP` in Charts.lua and ChartsRender.lua.

  The debug console and the launcher (LibDBIcon getAnchors) move too.
