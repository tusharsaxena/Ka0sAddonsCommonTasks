# #88 Adopt the v2.110.0 test/sim strip placement (under the title bar, HUD top edge) in every addon

## What
Adopt the v2.110.0 test/sim strip placement in every Ka0s addon when it re-vendors LibKa0s **v1.103.0** or later (Widgets minor 27).

The standard (WowAddonStandards v2.110.0, preview-mode "Where the strip sits", standalone-windows, anti-pattern #118) now requires:
- **Every window** that draws test or simulated data shows `TestStrip` (amber test, cyan `kind = "sim"`) **directly under the title bar**, full width, above tabs, filter bar and body. Build it with `opts.dock = "title"` (or `W.DockTestStrip`), and move the body down by `strip:BodyOffset()` / `onOffset`. The header shows no mode word while the strip shows. A minimized or header-hidden window keeps a header word or badge.
- **Every HUD display** in test or sim shows the strip docked to its **top edge, outside it** (`opts.dock = "hud"`), flipping under its bottom edge near the top of the screen. Call `strip:Place()` after the host moves the HUD. The simulation step panel stays. This retires the v2.93.0 HUD exemption and the "HUD MUST NOT carry a simulation marker" clause.

## Per addon
Re-vendor, move or add the strip in each window and HUD that shows placeholder data, remove any hand-anchored strip or header mode word, update the smoke checks and the docs. Mark an addon N/A if it has no such window or HUD. Current vendored tag in brackets.
