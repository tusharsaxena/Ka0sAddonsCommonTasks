# #85 Adopt LibKa0s-Callout-1.0 beyond Combat Trail: find the consumers

## Summary

A new module, **`LibKa0s-Callout-1.0`**, is being designed for Combat Trail's Race mode: a big centered countdown (up to 20 s) and one-off stock call-outs (READY, GO, START, STOP, WARNING, ERROR, DONE, NEW BEST, ...). It has configurable font (a bundled display font, the skin's font, or any LibSharedMedia font), size, outline flags, shadow, position, animations (slam, pulse, glow, blink, shake), and voice. The voice is either generated open-TTS packs (male and female) or the client's in-game TTS. Custom texts can carry an optional sound file. It also ships a settings section that consumers drop into their own panel.

Combat Trail is the first consumer. This issue tracks **where else in the collection it could be adopted** once it ships.

## Candidates to evaluate

- **QuickComms**: a strong candidate (call-outs and alerts). Check which of its alerts would read better as a centered callout with a voice line.
- **Addons that use `LibKa0s-Toast-1.0`**: DpsRace, DropWatcher, SkillMaster, QuickComms (and Combat Trail). For each, decide whether any toast is really an urgent, glanceable event (a countdown, a "now!" moment) that belongs in a callout, while the toast stays for the detail.
- **DpsRace**: it has its own race countdown, so it is the closest analogue.
- **Any other addon** with a timed "get ready" moment or an error or warning the player must not miss.

## For each candidate, record

- the event or events that would use it, as a countdown or as a stock or custom text;
- whether it needs custom sounds;
- whether the library's settings section fits its panel;
- the decision: adopt / not now / never.
