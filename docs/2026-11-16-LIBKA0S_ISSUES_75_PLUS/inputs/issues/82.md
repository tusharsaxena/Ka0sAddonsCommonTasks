# #82 WoW Forever skin: hover/hot highlight barely visible (icon.hot vs icon.tint / text.heading, list.hover)

## What

In the **WoW Forever** skin, a mark's hover (hot) state is hard to tell from its resting state. Owner feedback while testing Skill Master: the highlight is "barely visible today".

## Where it shows

- Icon controls tinted with `S.Mark`: they rest at `icon.tint` and go to `icon.hot` under the pointer, for example title-bar and block-header controls like Skill Master's detach mark and crafting-row hammer.
- In Forever, `icon.tint` is the muted `#A39E97` and `icon.hot` is the tan accent `#C9A07A`. Both are mid-light warm neutrals of about the same lightness, so the change reads as a slight hue shift, not a highlight.
- It gets worse when a host rests a mark at `text.heading` (Skill Master's block-header detach mark now matches its title). The heading's warm gold and the tan hot color are close to each other, so the hover nearly disappears.
- `list.hover` (a 0.15 wash) is worth checking alongside: a row's hover wash on Forever's warm charcoal may be just as faint.

## Ask

Make Forever's hover and hot states clearly visible:
- an `icon.hot` that stands well apart from both `icon.tint` and `text.heading` in lightness, not only in hue (white, or a much brighter gold, for example)
- a `list.hover` wash strong enough to read on the window background

A contrast check in the skin-values kit could hold this: hot against rest, and hot against heading, above a minimum lightness difference for every skin. Other skins may have the same gap.
